📋 1. LAB SUMMARY

"The Ghost in the Ledger is a simulated enterprise cybersecurity investigation.

A suspicious treasury transaction has moved 82,400 NXR to an unknown wallet.

The blockchain transaction itself appears valid, but the AI security system has classified the destination as trusted.

The investigation requires the learner to trace how untrusted intelligence, AI context, automated authorization, and Web3 transaction logic combined to create a dangerous trust chain.

The goal is not simply to find a compromised component.

The learner must discover how multiple individually trusted systems can produce an unsafe final decision."

🎯 2. LEARNING OBJECTIVES

The learner should understand:

  • Web3 transaction investigation
  • AI decision-making and confidence
  • Threat-intelligence poisoning
  • Trust-chain vulnerabilities
  • RBAC and authorization concepts
  • Automated transaction workflows
  • Security monitoring
  • Evidence correlation
  • Root-cause analysis
  • Defensive security controls
💼 3. BUSINESS IMPACT

A real-world version of this type of failure could result in:

  • Unauthorized financial transfers
  • Loss of digital assets
  • Incorrect automated decisions
  • Regulatory and compliance risk
  • Loss of customer trust
  • Operational disruption
  • Incident-response costs
  • Reputation damage
🔄 4. HOW IT WORKS

The investigation flow follows the enterprise trust chain where trust was introduced and became dangerous:

Threat Intelligence
↓
AI Context
↓
AI Decision
↓
Authorization Policy
↓
Automated Signing
↓
Web3 Transaction
↓
Blockchain Record
🛡️ 5. ATTACK SURFACE
  • Web3 wallet interactions: Unallowlisted destination wallets receiving high-value asset flows.
  • Threat-intelligence feeds: Ingestion pipelines accepting unverified external context feeds.
  • AI decision context: Decision engine context windows vulnerable to data poisoning.
  • Internal APIs: Microservice communication channels with excessive privilege scopes.
  • Authorization policies: Policy rules permitting automated settlement upon high confidence scores.
  • Automated transaction systems: Headless signing daemons lacking multisig checks.
  • Signing infrastructure: Key custody systems exposed to automated pipeline triggers.
  • Blockchain transaction layer: Irreversible on-chain settlement records.

  • Web3 forensics
  • AI security analysis
  • Threat intelligence analysis
  • Log analysis
  • Evidence correlation
  • Trust-boundary analysis
  • Incident investigation
  • Root-cause identification
🔍 7. INVESTIGATION OUTCOME
"How did trusted intelligence become a trusted AI decision, and how did that decision ultimately authorize an unauthorized Web3 transaction?"

"The Vanishing Consensus is a PRO-level cross-layer cybersecurity investigation involving connected IoT systems, AI security, Web3 data infrastructure, and distributed trust.

Several IoT devices begin producing unusually synchronized telemetry.

That telemetry enters a Web3 oracle/data layer.

🛡️ 5. ATTACK SURFACE
